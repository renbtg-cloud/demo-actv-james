# Milestone 164 — Reject forged script/checksum pairs

The launch contract reconstructs the script generated by the trusted planner. An arbitrary script, including one with its newly calculated SHA-256 inserted into the manifesto, fails the canonical replay rather than passing a self-consistent checksum pair.

Scope: offline software-level consistency on an operator-controlled trusted plan, not authenticated physical hosts, not a production benchmark, and not proof of universal exactly-once effects.
